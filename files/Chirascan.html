Rules

Always follow the turning ON/OFF procedure.

Turn on nitrogen flow at least 15 minutes before starting an experiment and keep it running for at least 15 minutes after switching off the lamp.

Daytime slots (08:00-17:00): if utilisation exceeded 75% over the previous 5 days, contact other users before booking more than 8 consecutive hours.

Evening and weekend slots: recommended for long experiments.
