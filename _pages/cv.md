Biography
======

I was born in Arad, Romania, in 1976. I attended the [West University of Timișoara](https://cbg.uvt.ro) where I received a B.Sc. degree in Chemistry in 1998 under the supervision of Dr. Ervin Salló. I continued my studies at the same university and obtained an M.Sc. degree in 2000 under the supervision of Prof. Zeno Simon. During my M.Sc. studies I was an Erasmus-Socrates graduate exchange student at [Bremen University](https://www.uni-bremen.de/en/) (1999-2000), working with Prof. Bernd Jastorff.

In August 2000, I moved to [The University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu) and conducted research in expanded-porphyrin and pyrrole-based anion recognition chemistry under the supervision of Prof. Jonathan Sessler, earning a Ph.D. in Chemistry in October 2005.

Between January 2006 and September 2007 I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the group of Prof. Jeremy Sanders at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk). In October 2007 I became a Stokes Research Fellow at [Pembroke College](https://www.pem.cam.ac.uk), Cambridge, while continuing my association with the Sanders Research Group. I joined the University of Bath as Lecturer in Organic Chemistry in January 2011 and became Senior Lecturer in May 2015.
