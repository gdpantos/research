# Come Join Our Team!

We are always interested in recruiting dedicated and talented scientists. If you are interested in working with us, please contact the group directly.

## Postdoctoral Positions

Applications for postdoctoral positions are considered on a rolling basis, subject to funding availability.

Applicants should:
- Hold a PhD, or
- Be within the final six months of doctoral studies in Chemistry, Biochemistry, or Materials Science.

Application packages should include:
- Cover letter
- Curriculum vitae (CV)
- Three reference letters from advisors or mentors familiar with your work

Researchers with independent funding or intending to apply for fellowships are encouraged to get in touch.

Funding for specific postdoctoral projects will be announced through group and departmental websites.

## PhD Positions

We typically consider PhD applications throughout the year.

Prospective applicants should send:
- CV
- Names of two academic referees willing to provide references

## MSc and BSc Students

Students interested in joining the group should contact us at the beginning of the academic year when projects are usually available.

Research projects provide opportunities to:
- Develop organic chemistry skills
- Develop analytical chemistry skills
- Gain first-hand experience in a research environment
- Support academic and professional development

## Summer, Erasmus and Visiting Students

We welcome visiting students and scientists interested in experiencing our research environment and collaborating with the group.
